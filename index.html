Our services

What we do

Four support services, each available on its own or combined into one plan.

01
Ongoing care

Extended support

Long-term, ongoing care for your IT. We become your outsourced support desk and look after users, devices and systems month after month.

  • Helpdesk with agreed response times
  • Patch, update and antivirus management
  • User account, email and access administration
  • Backup monitoring and monthly health reports
02
Short-term cover

Temporary support

Skilled technicians for the periods when you need extra hands: staff leave, office moves, audits, rollouts or sudden spikes in tickets.

  • Short-term engineers by the day, week or month
  • Event, project and migration cover
  • Quick start with minimal onboarding
  • Clean handover notes when we finish
03
Repair & upgrade

Hardware support

Repair, setup and maintenance for the physical equipment your team relies on every day.

  • Desktop, laptop and printer repair
  • Peripheral, RAM, SSD and component upgrades
  • New system setup and asset tagging
  • Router, switch and network device configuration
04
Install & fix

Software support

We install, configure and fix the software your business depends on, from the operating system to everyday applications.

  • Windows installation, repair and upgrades
  • Application deployment and licence setup
  • Remote troubleshooting and VPN / RDP access
  • Malware cleanup and data backup or recovery
